Account Deletion

How to delete your Rocket Life account and associated account data.

Delete from Rocket Life

Open Rocket Life, go to the Account area, choose Delete Account, and follow the confirmation steps shown in the app. A Rocket Games account is optional for normal gameplay.

If you cannot access the app

Send a deletion request from the email address associated with your account when possible. Use the subject Rocket Life Account Deletion.

Request deletion by email

What to include

  • The email address associated with the Rocket Life account.
  • A clear request to delete the account.
  • Do not send passwords, authentication codes, private save files or other unnecessary sensitive information.

How the request is handled

Rocket Games may need to verify that the request belongs to the account holder before processing it. The deletion process covers the account and account-linked data handled by the current Rocket Life deletion flow. Public community content may require separate review when it is no longer directly associated with an active account.

Privacy information

See the Rocket Life Privacy Policy for more information about data handling.
