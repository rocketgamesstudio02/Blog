Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

Overview

Rocket Life is developed by Rocket Games. Core gameplay can be used without creating a Rocket Games account. Optional online features use supported third-party services for sign-in, Cloud Saves, community features, release delivery, diagnostics, consent management and advertising.

On-device game data

Normal life simulation progress is stored on your device. If you choose Cloud Saves, supported save data may also be stored online and associated with your account so it can be backed up or restored.

Account information

If you use a Rocket Life account, authentication services may process information needed to sign you in, such as account identifiers and information supplied by the sign-in method you choose.

Cloud Saves, Mods and community features

When you use Cloud Saves, Mod Studio or community-connected features, information you choose to upload and related technical metadata may be stored online to provide, secure and manage those features. Content you intentionally publish may be visible to other users.

Firebase, diagnostics and advertising

Rocket Life uses supported Google/Firebase services for online functionality and may use Google advertising services. Depending on the feature, device settings and consent choices, these services may process technical, diagnostic, consent, device or advertising-related information under their own terms and privacy practices.

Account deletion

Account users can request deletion through Rocket Life's account controls. If you cannot access the app, see the Account Deletion page.
