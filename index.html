About this game

Shape a life, year by year

Simulation Single player Offline progress Choice-based

Live through childhood, school, adulthood and old age while making choices that affect your character’s future. Build relationships, pursue careers, manage money, own assets, take risks and deal with the consequences of every decision.

Guide your character’s life Study, work, build a family, manage health and make decisions that shape every year.
Explore detailed life systems Relationships, finances, prison, crime, education, jobs, houses, vehicles and more.
Experience long-term consequences Your choices carry forward into future years, affecting opportunities, stress, success and failure.
