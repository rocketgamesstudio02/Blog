About this game

Your choices build the story

Rocket Life is a native Android life simulator built around long-term decisions rather than short, disconnected events. Start a life, grow through school and adulthood, pursue work and education, build relationships, manage finances and property, and deal with the consequences of what your character chooses to do.

The simulation includes family and social relationships, education and careers, money and assets, health, crime and prison systems, major life events, and character progression across an entire lifetime. The game is independently developed by Rocket Games and is designed to keep expanding over time.

Data Safety

How data is handled

Rocket Life keeps core gameplay and save progress on your device. Some online services used by the app, such as Firebase and advertising services, may process limited technical, diagnostic, consent, or advertising-related information required to provide those features.

✓

◎
No account required to playThe core game does not require creating a Rocket Games account before starting a life.
